# People

## Finding someone

Type an `@username`, a `name@hashgram.io` address, or a `hash1…` address.
Usernames are looked up on chain; the answer shows how many active devices
the identity has. Someone with no device on chain has not registered yet
and cannot receive mail until they do.

## Contacts

A **contact request** travels encrypted to the other person. When they
accept, you are contacts: mail between you goes to the Inbox instead of
Requests, and their posts appear in the **Friends** tab of Feed. Contact
state is private to your devices; it is never on chain.

## Follow

Following is a **public** event (anyone can see who follows whom). It
puts someone's public posts in your **Following** tab.

## Block, mute, trust

**Block** drops everything from an address. **Mute** keeps mail but stops
notifications and lowers its priority. **Trust** marks a contact whose
mail should never be filtered. All three are local.

## Your card

**Send my card** gives a contact your display name and bio privately.
Ticking *disclose wallet address* includes your `hash1…` so they can pay
you; leave it off and your contact sees only your username.

## Two names

Your **public** display name and bio go on the network for everyone
(Feed profile). Your **private** display name goes only into mail headers
and cards you send. They can differ.
